Practice FTC before the robot is ready.

BIOBUZZ is a 3D FTC field simulator: drive with a keyboard or gamepad, test your autonomous, play full matches against bots or friends, and track every driver's stats.

⬇ Download for Windows Portable version (no install)
Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit · free · English and Hebrew

Install in one minute

  1. Download BIOBUZZ-Setup.exe with the button above.
  2. Run it. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because the app is not code-signed yet — click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Pick a folder (or keep the default) and click Install. A desktop shortcut is created.
  4. Open BIOBUZZ, type your name and (optionally) your team name and number. A one-minute tour shows you around.
  5. Updates are automatic — the app checks on every launch and offers to install new versions.

Prefer not to install? The portable version runs straight from the file. Your data stays on your computer; signing in to an account (optional) syncs drivers and matches across computers.
